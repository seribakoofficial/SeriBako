Our Story

A Heritage Worth Preserving

Seri Bako was born from the legacy of Kwan Kee — one of Penang's most beloved heritage kuih makers. What began as a family craft passed down through generations has grown into a Michelin-recognised name, celebrated for its commitment to authenticity.

"Every piece of kuih carries a memory. We are merely its custodians."

We believe that great kuih is not just food — it is culture, memory, and grace on a plate. Each recipe is preserved with intention, each ingredient chosen with care.
